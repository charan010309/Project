Privacy

Manage your conversations.

Conversations are stored in your account history. You can view, export, or delete them and manage signed-in devices. Messages are sent to the AI provider you select; that provider's data practices also apply. An ephemeral mode is not currently available.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own AI provider?

You can connect a supported OpenAI-compatible provider and choose an available model. Your provider may charge for usage under its own terms.

Are conversations saved?

Chat and voice conversations are stored in your account history. You can review or delete them; ephemeral mode is not currently available.

Can I use voice and images?

You can attach images for Gemini analysis and use voice where browser speech support and permissions are available.
